Our Services

Comprehensive Podiatry Services in Astoria, NY

From surgical procedures to laser treatments, we offer complete foot and ankle care under one roof.

Bunion Surgery

Our expert podiatrists specialize in advanced bunion surgery techniques to correct painful deformities, realign your foot structure, and restore comfortable mobility. We customize every surgical approach to your specific condition and lifestyle needs.

Ingrown Toenail Removal & Surgery

We provide expert ingrown toenail removal and surgical solutions to permanently resolve this painful condition. Our precise, minimally invasive techniques ensure quick healing and prevent recurrence so you can walk pain-free again.

Foot Fungus Laser Treatment

Our advanced foot fungus laser therapy provides a safe, non-invasive, and highly effective solution for stubborn toenail fungus. Eliminate discoloration and restore healthy, clear nails without the side effects of oral medications.

Foot & Ankle Pain Management

Our comprehensive foot and ankle pain management services identify and treat the root cause of your discomfort. Whether from injury, overuse, or chronic conditions, we develop a targeted treatment plan to restore your mobility and quality of life.

Diabetic Foot Care

Specialized diabetic foot care is essential to prevent serious complications. Our team provides preventive screenings, wound care, neuropathy management, and education to protect your foot health and help you maintain an active lifestyle.

Sports Injury Treatment

Athletes and active individuals trust us for expert sports injury treatment. From sprains and fractures to tendonitis, we provide accurate diagnosis, targeted rehabilitation, and return-to-play guidance for optimal recovery.

Heel Pain & Plantar Fasciitis Treatment

Chronic heel pain and plantar fasciitis can severely impact your daily life. We offer proven treatment options including custom orthotics, stretching therapy, and advanced interventions to relieve pain and restore comfortable walking.
